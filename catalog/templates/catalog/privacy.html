Privacy

DSAI Vault is a study site for one university programme, run by a student. It is not an official university service. This page says what it keeps about you and how to remove it.

What is stored

  • From Google, when you sign in: your name, your e-mail address and Google's identifier for your account. Nothing else is requested: no contacts, no files, no calendar, and never your password.
  • Your study progress: which courses you put on your dashboard, how you graded each flashcard and question, how you rated each lecture, your mock exam scores, and how many cards and focus minutes you did per day.
  • A session cookie, which keeps you signed in for up to 60 days, and a second cookie that protects forms against forgery.
  • In your own browser: a working copy of your progress, so the site also works offline, and your theme and timer settings.

What it is used for

To show you your own progress on every device you sign in on. That is the only use. There are no advertisements and no analytics, and nothing is sold or passed to anyone.

Where it is

On the site's own server. Requests reach it through Cloudflare, which as the network provider sees the address your request comes from. Copies of the database are kept for about two weeks so that a failure does not lose everyone's progress.

Removing it

Open Settings and choose Delete my account. Your account and all progress saved to it are removed from the server at once, and from the remaining backup copies as they are replaced over the following two weeks. Clearing progress for one course, or all of it, is on the same page.

What you may expect of the site, and it of you, is in the terms of use.
