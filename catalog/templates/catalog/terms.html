Terms of use

What your lecturers say and hand out is what counts. Nothing here promises what an exam will ask, and the site is not responsible for a mark you get after studying from it.

What it is for

For your own study. Read it, drill it, print a sheet for yourself. Do not republish it, sell it, or copy the site wholesale, and do not load it with scripts harder than a person studying would.
