# source/

Raw course material, exactly as you received it. Nothing in here is shown on the site and
nothing in here is edited by content-writing sessions: it is what they read.

    lectures/   slides and lecture PDFs
    labs/       lab sheets, notebooks, starter code
    homework/   assignment sheets and your submissions
    exams/      past papers, photos of papers, exam-prep handouts
    misc/       anything else: syllabus, textbook chapters, your own notes

File names are free-form. A lecture note lists the files it was written from in its
`sources:` field; `manage.py content_status` shows which files here nothing refers to yet.
