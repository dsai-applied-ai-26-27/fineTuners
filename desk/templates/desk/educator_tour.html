You are an educator now

You write one course for everyone who studies it here. Your page is Educators in the menu. It shows what is waiting, and a card for each course you write. A course's own page has its files, the form that takes your work, what was added so far, and what students asked about it.

git clone …/dsai-educator-kit.git
git pull        # before each session

Get the educator kit

A public repository with the instructions your AI assistant follows, the format of every file, and three small tools. It holds no course. Clone it once; git pull brings each new version, and you are told when there is one.

uv run tools/unpack.py rl.zip
# courses/rl/ is now what the site has

Start from what the site has

Download your course's files on the Educators page and unpack them into the kit. That is your starting point, kept by git on your computer only. Then put the slides and papers in the course's source/ folder; they never leave your computer.

Update the CMS for this course: rl

Let your assistant write. Then check it

Open the kit in your AI assistant and give it that line. It writes notes, cards and problems from your material. Reading them against the lecture is your part: an assistant writes mistakes as confidently as everything else.

Pack it and hand it in

uv run tools/pack.py rl checks your course and makes one zip. Upload it on your course's page. You may add, change and remove; what a hand-in removes is shown back to you first, and you confirm it with a tick. If it passes the site's check it is live at once, and every earlier version is kept.

git stash
uv run tools/unpack.py rl.zip
git stash pop

If the course moved on without you

When someone else added to your course, the site refuses a zip built on the older version and shows these steps: set your work aside, bring in the latest, put your work back on top. Every step, in full, is in the guide. This tour stays in Settings.
