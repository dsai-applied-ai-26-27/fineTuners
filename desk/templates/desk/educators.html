  1. Get the educator kit. It holds the instructions for your AI assistant, the format and the tools, and no course content: {{ kit }}. Clone it once; run git pull in it before each session.

What it takes

The work. For each lecture: notes that can be read in one sitting, flashcards, and where the course has them, problems and past papers with worked answers. All of it is plain text files in a fixed format, written on your own computer.

The tools. It can be done by hand or with a free chatbot. For a whole course, what works is a paid AI assistant that reads the slides and writes the files, with you checking every lecture against the original. The guide shows the whole way, from the first clone to the hand-in.

What you get. The course you know best, in a form your whole year can study from, and requests about it come to you.
