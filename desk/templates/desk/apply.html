Before you apply

How a course gets written. The content is plain text files in a fixed format, kept in a shared repository. Nothing is typed into the website. You can write the files by hand, or with a free chatbot, and for a lecture or two that works. For a whole course the realistic way is a paid AI assistant that can read the slides and write files on your computer (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or similar, about 20 € a month). You hand it a lecture, it writes in the site's format from the instructions in the repository, and you check what it wrote against the lecture.

What stays with you. The checking. What goes up under your course has to be right, and an assistant gets things wrong with a straight face. Plan an hour or two per lecture.

What happens next. The person who runs the site reads your application and contacts you on the number you give. If it is a yes, you get access to the course files and a short walk through the first lecture.
