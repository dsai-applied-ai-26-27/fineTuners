Read a note, then rate yourself

Every lecture is written up as a note. At the end of it, say how well you know it: lost, shaky or confident. An honest answer is what makes the progress bars worth looking at.

Grade every flashcard

Answer out loud, reveal, then grade yourself. A missed card comes straight back, a shaky one tomorrow, a solid one after 1, 3, 7, 14 and then 30 days. On a keyboard, space reveals and 1 2 3 grade.
