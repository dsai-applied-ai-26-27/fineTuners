Start with what is next

The dashboard always names one thing to do: a lecture you have not read, cards that are due, the next step of an exam plan. Not sure where to begin? Begin there.

Read a note, then rate yourself

Every lecture is written up as a note. At the end of it, say how well you know it: lost, shaky or confident. An honest answer is what makes the progress bars worth looking at.

Grade every flashcard

Answer out loud, reveal, then grade yourself. A missed card comes straight back, a shaky one tomorrow, a solid one after 1, 3, 7, 14 and then 30 days. On a keyboard, space reveals and 1 2 3 grade.

Practise before the exam

Problems keep their solutions closed until you ask for them. A past paper can be read with worked answers or sat against the clock. Each exam has a page saying what it covers and how much each topic weighs.

Missing something? Say so

Ask or report in the menu is one short form: content a course lacks, a mistake you found, an idea for the site. The answer comes back on the same page. The courses are written by students, so a course with nothing in it yet also lets you apply to become its educator and write it. This tour stays in Settings, beside what is new on the site.
